Barthélémy Vielle — art director and graphic designer, based between Beaucens and Cauterets, French Pyrenees. Art direction, branding, motion, signage, visual systems.

CLVSTR is my practice: a cluster of stars — distant objects perceived as close. Black and white as the connective thread, color as an event.

Under it: Baruto, the client-facing side for identity branding (restaurants, hotels, brands). Rockaedron, products and objects. Forgeworks, a separate studio.
